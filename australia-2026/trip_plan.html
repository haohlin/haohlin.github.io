Why Cairns & surrounds in winter

为什么 凯恩斯与周边 是黄金选择

Your summer is North Queensland's dry season — counter-intuitively, the best time to be there. Bookings are now locked in: 5 nights in Port Douglas for the rainforest + reef portion (Sheraton Grand Mirage 26–28 Jul, then Wyndham Ramada 28–31 Jul), followed by 4 nights at Crystalbrook Bailey in Cairns (31 Jul – 4 Aug) for Kuranda, a whale-watching-first Fitzroy Island day, and a Mission Beach skydive. A Hyundai rental car bridges the FNQ leg out of Cairns Airport. One outer-reef day, humpback whale watching + Fitzroy Island, the famous Daintree + Cape Tribulation day trip, and a tandem skydive over the Great Barrier Reef anchor the trip.

你这边的夏天正是北昆士兰的干季 — 反而是去那里的最佳时间。预订已全部锁定：道格拉斯港 5 晚承接雨林 + 大堡礁部分（7/26–28 喜来登 Mirage，7/28–31 Wyndham Ramada），然后 凯恩斯 Crystalbrook Bailey 4 晚（7/31 – 8/4），用来去库兰达、先观鲸后上 Fitzroy Island，以及 Mission Beach 跳伞。一辆 Hyundai 租车从凯恩斯机场提取衔接 FNQ 段。一次外礁、座头鲸观鲸 + Fitzroy Island、戴恩树 + 苦难角一日游、大堡礁上空双人跳伞，构成行程主轴。
